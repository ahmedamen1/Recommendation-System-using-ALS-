BEHIND THE RECOMMENDATIONS

About your model

These recommendations use the movie vectors and biases exported from your trained notebook.

What happens when you rate a film?

We fit a new user vector to your ratings, keep the learned movie parameters fixed, and score the remaining catalog. Movies you’ve already rated are excluded.

Predicted stars are model estimates, clipped to the 0.5–5 scale for display. Shared-genre notes describe overlap with your liked films; they are not a causal explanation of the model.

To use your Colab model, start the app with --bundle path/to/inference_bundle.zip.
